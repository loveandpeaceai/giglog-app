2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Concert Information: Event name, artist, date, time, venue, seat details, ticket price, etc., that you manually enter
  • Ticket Photos: Photos of concert tickets that you take or upload
  • Live Photos & Videos: Photos and videos (including Live Photos) taken at concert venues
  • Personal Reviews: Ratings, comments, and thoughts about the concerts you attend

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Anonymous device model, iOS version, and app version to ensure app compatibility
  • Usage Data: Anonymous statistics on app feature usage frequency to optimize user experience
  • Crash Logs: Anonymous app crash information to fix bugs and improve stability

3. Permissions We Request and Their Purposes

3.1 Photo Library Access

  • Purpose: Save concert ticket photos and live photos to your album, or import existing photos from your library
  • Data Handling: All photos are stored only on your device and iCloud (if you enable iCloud sync)
  • Uploaded to Servers: No
  • Optional: You can refuse photo library access and only use the camera feature

3.2 Camera Access

  • Purpose: Scan ticket information (OCR recognition) and take live photos/videos at concerts
  • Data Handling: All captured content is saved only to your device locally
  • Uploaded to Servers: No
  • Optional: You can refuse camera access and manually enter all concert information

3.3 Location Access

  • Purpose: Record the geographic location of concert venues for easy viewing and management on maps
  • Data Handling: Location data is stored only on your device locally
  • Uploaded to Servers: No
  • Optional: You can refuse location access and manually enter venue information

3.4 Notification Permissions

  • Purpose: Send concert countdown reminders and Live Activity updates
  • Data Handling: All notifications are generated and displayed locally on your device
  • Uploaded to Servers: No
  • Optional: You can refuse notification permissions without affecting core app functionality

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All your data (including concert information, photos, locations, etc.) is stored by default only on your device locally, managed using the SwiftData database.

4.2 iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you enable iCloud sync:

  • Data is synced through Apple's iCloud service
  • Data is stored in your personal iCloud account
  • We have no access to your iCloud data
  • Data transmission and storage are protected by Apple's security mechanisms

4.3 Data Encryption

  • The app supports encrypted data backup functionality
  • Industry-standard AES-256 encryption algorithm is used to protect your backup data

4.4 Data Retention Period

  • All your data will remain permanently on your device and iCloud account until you actively delete it
  • When you delete the app, locally stored data will be permanently deleted, but data in iCloud will remain and must be manually deleted in your iCloud settings
  • We do not retain any copies of your data on our servers

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We absolutely will NOT:

  • Sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Analyze your behavioral data for commercial use

Data may be disclosed only in the following circumstances:

  • To comply with laws, regulations, or court orders
  • To protect our legitimate rights and interests
  • To prevent fraud or security threats
